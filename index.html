Parker Plott Photo

Hi! My name is Parker! I’m happy you are here.

I am a photographer in Newport News, VA with a soft spot for photographing in my own neighborhood, Hilton Village.

Originally born and raised in San Diego, I have photographed for over ten years focusing primarily in Tennessee, Alabama, and now Virginia. I have covered events including weddings and concerts. I have done most iterations of portrait photography work including family photos, senior pictures, and business headshots.

My work has been published in The Daily Beacon, Preier Christianity, and on advertising billboards in the Nashville area. I have been fortunate to work with Grammy-Award winning Musicians, New York Times Bestselling authors, and various businesses across the states.

I am a strong believer that there is “no bad light” in photography meaning that there is no logistical hurdle I am unwilling to face. I am approachable, fun, playful, and love meeting new people. I love capturing joy, beauty, love, and everything else that makes us human.
